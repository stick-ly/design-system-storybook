# Design exploration archive

> Historical source material. Preserved on 2026-10-02, not current implementation or release authorization.

Design decisions benefit from keeping their context and rejected alternatives.
These dated records are preserved unchanged, including qualifications,
superseded checklists, machine-local provenance and implementation limits.
Current product direction belongs in the living product handbook; the runtime
catalog is the authority for current producer component presentation.
