### Independent specialist synthesis — narrowed scope

The learning-science specialist and interaction critic both completed a second pass on the user's in-place refinement, independently of the initial broad ideas.

- **Default recommendation:** one quiet disclosure, one paired example, one useful cue. Translation remains immediate; missing enrichment does not create a spinner or disabled promise. The question-driven pattern is most appropriate for an explicitly saved Smart Translate result; the recall/reveal pattern belongs inside current review. Do not pile all three patterns into every component.
- **Relevance priority:** exact sense first, then a reusable chunk/collocation, then one relevant confusion or structural cue. Morphology appears when it is useful for this word, not because its field exists. “Frequency” must not be asserted without an actual frequency source; the pack can propose a useful chunk but does not establish corpus frequency.
- **Interaction discipline:** only questions with real cached answers appear. One answer pane is open at a time. Close returns to the same compact surface; expansion does not steal focus. AI supplemental content stays hidden while it would disclose a pending answer.
- **Learning claim:** retrieval before corrective feedback has support from the cited retrieval literature. That does not prove that this exact pane design, reading examples, or immediate reconstruction improves delayed learning. Context-variability findings are mixed; begin with one clear sense-matched example rather than maximizing novelty.
- **Evidence and grading:** a deliberate unrevealed attempt is different from listening, opening notes, viewing a model, or reconstructing an already-shown phrase. Those latter actions do not award recall success. Optional post-answer masking is assisted practice and must not produce a second grade or scheduling update. A cloze does not establish general productive mastery.
- **Evaluation:** measure delayed unseen-context understanding and appropriate use per active minute, while preserving translation readiness and zero required extra taps. Reading-resume and exit behavior are guardrails. Specify numeric thresholds through a powered experiment and product agreement, not arbitrary claims from a mockup.

No independent specialist characterized these generated images as tested learning improvements. Their recommendations concern mechanisms and interaction hierarchy; the selected runnable prototype still needs user iteration and visual/interaction critique.

## Information budget

1. Before recall: the task and enough context to understand it; not its answer.
2. After recall: correct meaning, one example/chunk, one selected cue if it materially helps.
3. Only on request: additional contexts, morphology, register, other senses, exhaustive reference detail.
4. Save/scheduling: one quiet truthful status, with prominence only when action is required.
5. Progress: distinguish encountered, self-reported, assisted, recalled, and produced. Do not label one success “mastered,” use a knowledge percentage, or treat passive reading as a review.

Candidate cue precedence is conditional, not a fixed dictionary-field list: repair a just-observed verified error; otherwise choose the pack's relevant usage tip or collocation; otherwise give one short example. Do not invent diagnosis from letter mistakes. Omit a cue when there is no reliable useful content.

If selected, optional masking inside the existing review can hide a validated contiguous example span and later reveal its paired meaning. Omit that optional action when no suitable span exists. It must remain local, assisted, unscored practice: no scheduler call, extra grade, change to accepted translations, or claim of general productive mastery. Default example/cue expansion does not require masking or a new assessment contract.

Question-driven details read precomputed pack fields; a question click must not invoke a provider. Only supported usage, meaning, or form questions appear. Smart Translate resolves each saved result's exact word/pack link, so one language result cannot borrow another result's enrichment. Unsaved, pending, unsupported, or missing links retain today's translation/actions. Free composition, semantic AI grading, and associated APIs are out of scope.

**Content-subject/language gate:** the current provider instructs definition, sense, usage, tips, and morphology feature values in the target language and sets `explanationLanguage` to that language (`backend/functions/src/LearningPack/provider.ts`). `LearningPackV1` has one unqualified morphology/usage object, not separately attributed source- and target-lexeme annotations. The English explanatory prose shown beneath a German result in the image is therefore illustrative, not guaranteed cached content. Render explanations in their declared language; do not silently translate or regenerate them on click. Explicitly identify which word/phrase a cue or form describes. A target-specific question may appear only when its content is proven to describe that target expression; otherwise omit the question or label the actual source subject. Resolving ambiguous lexical attribution is a contract/content-quality gate, not a reason to invent available data or authorize a second generation call.

No schema widening merely to fill a screen. No global dictionary of personal drafts. No duplicate AI call during translation. No generation enablement, deploy, or new credentials in this exploration.

## Learning hypotheses and evaluation plan

These are proposed product experiments, not proven claims that the new UI teaches faster. The first row evaluates the selected option; the question-pane and masking comparisons remain unselected research ideas and do not authorize implementation in this slice.

| Hypothesis | Comparison | Learning outcome | Guardrail |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| One example plus a selected cue is more useful than a full detail list | One useful layer inside the existing bubble/result/review versus current expansion, with matched access | Delayed sense recall and appropriate use per practice minute | Translation readiness, required taps, reading-resume time, dismissals |
| Content-specific questions improve relevance without overload | The question you have versus a single default disclosure in saved Smart Translate rows | Delayed understanding plus finding the needed usage/form information | Time to answer, unused controls, unnecessary expansion, wrong-sense reports |
| Optional masking adds value beyond reading the same example | Existing review with versus without optional unscored masking, controlling content/time | Delayed phrase recall and unseen-context understanding, separately reported | Extra effort, opt-out, next-word friction, no duplicate scheduler updates |

Use learner-level randomized assignment or a suitably counterbalanced design, preregister a primary outcome and stopping rule, and power the comparison before making a superiority claim. Evaluate delayed performance, not just completion or immediate success. Do not upload personal text for analytics; collect bounded event categories/timing. Human-rated research samples require separate explicit participation and data handling.
