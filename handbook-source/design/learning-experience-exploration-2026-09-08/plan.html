Non-negotiable boundaries

No translation latency added. No full packs under eagerly loaded user words. Shared synthetic sense material, private captured text, no inferred browsing interests. Questions use proven lexical subjects and declared explanation languages, without per-click model calls. Optional masking is separate unscored practice, never another SRS grade. Report ships only with a functioning private flow. No deployment or generation enablement during exploration.

What would make the design successful?

Better delayed recall and appropriate novel-context use per minute, without increased reading interruption. Completion rates, scores, and attractive screenshots are supporting evidence—not proof of learning quality. The full brief specifies comparison conditions and guardrails.
